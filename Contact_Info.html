What can you contact us about?

Use this page to suggest a new tool, report a bug, flag a content or calculation issue, ask a privacy question, send a general inquiry, or reach out about business and advertising opportunities.

Reporting a tool problem

The most useful bug reports include:

  • The tool name or page URL.
  • Your browser and device type.
  • The steps that produced the problem.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • Any visible error message, copied exactly when possible.

Reporting a content or calculation issue

If you believe a formula, explanation, source link, assumption, or result is incorrect, tell us which page and which part you are referring to.

Tool suggestions

Describe the task you want the utility to solve, the inputs you would provide, and the result you would want the tool to produce.

Privacy and data questions

You can ask how a specific Utiliverse tool processes information or which third-party service a feature uses. See the Privacy Policy for current site-wide disclosures.

Please do not send sensitive secrets through this form. Do not include passwords, private keys, authentication tokens, payment-card details, government identification numbers, medical records, or other highly sensitive information. Redact confidential values before pasting logs, headers, URLs, or error output.
Utiliverse reviews messages for support, corrections, tool suggestions, privacy questions, and site operations. Response time can vary by message type and volume, and submitting the form does not create a service-level or response-time guarantee.
