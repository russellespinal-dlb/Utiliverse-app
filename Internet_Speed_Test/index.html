How this internet speed test works

A browser speed test estimates how quickly data can move between your device and an external measurement endpoint. Download speed measures data arriving at your browser, while upload speed measures data being transmitted from your browser. Ping measures round-trip delay, and jitter describes the variation between latency samples.
