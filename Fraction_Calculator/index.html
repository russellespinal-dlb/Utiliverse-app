How to Use the Fraction Calculator

This fraction calculator handles the most common fraction operations in one place. You can add, subtract, multiply or divide proper fractions, improper fractions and mixed numbers. The result is reduced to simplest form and can also be displayed as a mixed number and decimal when appropriate.

Add and subtract fractions

When two fractions have different denominators, they must be expressed using a common denominator before their numerators can be combined. For example, 1/3 + 1/4 can be rewritten as 4/12 + 3/12, which equals 7/12. The calculator performs the equivalent arithmetic automatically and reduces the final answer when possible.

Mixed numbers are supported as well. Enter the whole-number portion in the Whole field and the fractional portion in the numerator and denominator fields. A value such as 2 1/2 is converted internally to an improper fraction for the calculation, then presented in a readable form in the result.

Multiply fractions

To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators together and multiply the denominators together. For example, 2/3 × 3/5 produces 6/15, which simplifies to 2/5. The calculator automatically reduces the product to lowest terms using the greatest common divisor.

Divide fractions

Dividing by a fraction is equivalent to multiplying by its reciprocal. For example, 3/4 ÷ 2/5 becomes 3/4 × 5/2, which equals 15/8 or 1 7/8. A denominator of zero is not valid, and this calculator blocks undefined division-by-zero operations.

Decimal to fraction calculator

The Decimal to Fraction tab converts a terminating decimal into an equivalent fraction. A decimal such as 0.75 represents 75 hundredths, or 75/100. Dividing the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor reduces that value to 3/4. Likewise, 3.125 becomes 3125/1000 and simplifies to 25/8, which is also 3 1/8.

The converter uses the number of decimal places you enter to build the denominator as a power of ten, then reduces the fraction. Because the input is finite, repeating decimals such as 0.333... are treated according to the exact digits entered rather than as an infinite repeating value.

Fraction to decimal calculator

Converting a fraction to a decimal is done by dividing the numerator by the denominator. For example, 3/4 equals 0.75 and 5/8 equals 0.625. You can also enter a mixed number such as 2 1/4, which converts to 2.25.

What does simplest form mean?

A fraction is in simplest form, or lowest terms, when the numerator and denominator no longer share a common factor greater than one. For example, 12/18 simplifies to 2/3 because both 12 and 18 can be divided by 6. Reducing fractions makes equivalent values easier to compare and use in later calculations.

Common uses for fraction calculations

Fraction math appears in recipes, measurements, carpentry, construction, schoolwork, engineering and everyday comparisons. A cook may need to double 3/4 cup, a carpenter may add several fractional-inch measurements, or a student may need to verify a mixed-number calculation. Decimal conversion is also useful when a measurement written as a fraction must be entered into software that expects decimal values.
