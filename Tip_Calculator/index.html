Free Online Tip Calculator

The Utiliverse Tip Calculator provides a fast way to calculate gratuity, tax, service charges, final bill total, and the amount each person should pay when a check is shared. Enter the bill amount, choose a common tip percentage or type a custom percentage, and the results update instantly. You can also add sales tax, account for an existing service charge, choose whether gratuity is based on the pre-tax or post-tax amount, and split the finished total between multiple people.

All calculations happen locally in your browser. No account is required and the bill amount is not sent to a Utiliverse server. The tool works well for restaurants, delivery orders, taxis, salons, hotel services, tours, and other situations where you want to estimate a percentage-based gratuity or divide a payment quickly.

How to Use the Tip Calculator

Enter the bill amount

Start with the amount you want to use as the base of the calculation. In a restaurant this is often the food-and-drink subtotal before gratuity. If the receipt already includes tax and you do not want to separate it, enter the displayed amount and leave the tax field at zero. Decimal values are supported, so an amount such as 87.43 can be entered exactly.

Select the currency symbol

Choose the symbol that matches the bill. This setting changes how results are displayed and does not perform exchange-rate conversion. Percentage mathematics is the same in every currency, so travelers can simply choose the local symbol and calculate the gratuity in that currency.

Choose a common or custom tip

Quick buttons are provided for 10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, 22%, and 25%. Selecting one updates the custom-tip field instantly. You can also type any non-negative percentage, including decimal values. Setting the custom value to zero removes additional gratuity entirely, which can be useful when a service charge is already included.

Add tax when needed

The tax field is optional. Enter the percentage if the bill field contains a pre-tax subtotal and you want a complete estimate. Tax is calculated from the original bill amount. If the amount you entered already includes tax, leave the tax field at zero so the same tax is not counted twice.

Account for a service charge

Some restaurants, hotels, event venues, and large-party checks include a service charge automatically. Enter that percentage separately so it appears in the breakdown. A service charge and an optional tip are not always the same thing, so read the receipt carefully before deciding whether to add more gratuity.

Tip before or after tax

By default, the tool calculates tip from the original bill amount. Enable Tip After Tax if you prefer to calculate gratuity from the subtotal plus tax. The option makes the calculation transparent and lets you match your preferred method rather than assuming one approach.

Split the bill

Use the plus and minus controls to set the number of people sharing the payment. The calculator divides the final total evenly and also shows the tip portion per person. For example, a $120 total split among four people becomes $30 each. If people owe different amounts, a dedicated bill splitter is more appropriate.

Round for convenience

Round Total Up moves the complete bill to the next whole currency unit. Round Each Person's Share Up separately rounds the per-person amount. These options can make cash payments simpler, although rounding each person's share upward can cause the combined payments to exceed the exact bill slightly.

Practical Uses

Restaurant dining

Enter the meal subtotal, select a tip percentage, include tax if necessary, and review the gratuity and total. Groups splitting evenly can increase the number of people for an instant per-person amount.

Food delivery and takeout

Delivery orders may contain delivery fees, service fees, taxes, and an optional driver tip. Use the amount that best matches the portion you want to tip on, and use the service-charge field when you want an existing percentage-based fee shown separately.

Travel and hospitality

Tipping customs vary widely. Travelers can use the calculator for taxis, tours, hotel services, restaurant meals, or other percentage-based gratuities, while checking local customs and whether a service charge is already included.

Salons and personal services

Haircuts, nail services, spa appointments, and similar services are often tipped by percentage where gratuities are customary. Enter the service amount and desired percentage to see both gratuity and final payment quickly.

Large parties and automatic gratuity

If an automatic gratuity or service charge is already on the check, enter it in the service-charge field. You can set additional tip to zero or choose another percentage if you intentionally want to add more.

Checking mental math

Estimating 10% and doubling it is a convenient way to approximate a 20% tip. The calculator is more useful when the bill contains cents, the percentage is unusual, tax or service charges need to be separated, or several people want an exact split.

Tip Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate a 20% tip?

Multiply the bill by 0.20. For example, 20% of $50 is $10.

Should I tip before or after tax?

Many people use the pre-tax subtotal, but practices vary. This tool supports either method.

Can I split the bill?

Yes. Increase the number of people to see total and tip per person.

What if a service charge is included?

Enter it separately and decide whether you want to add any additional tip.

Does the currency selector convert exchange rates?

No. It changes the displayed currency symbol only.

Does Utiliverse upload my bill amount?

No. All calculations are performed locally in the browser.

Can I calculate a zero-percent tip?

Yes. Enter 0 in the custom-tip field.

Why can rounded per-person totals be higher?

Rounding each share upward independently can make the combined amount slightly exceed the exact total.
