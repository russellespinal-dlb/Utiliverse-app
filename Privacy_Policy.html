Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Utiliverse, we believe in minimizing data collection. Many of our utility tools are designed to execute entirely within your web browser. For example, text processing and typing test inputs are not transmitted to or stored on our servers. When you visit our website, standard standard server logs (such as IP addresses, browser types, and timestamps) may be automatically collected to ensure network security and monitor site performance.

2. Third-Party Services and APIs

Some of our tools rely on external infrastructure to function correctly. When using these specific tools, your requests may interact directly with third-party providers:

  • Cloudflare: Tools designed to test network latency and speeds may route traffic through Cloudflare servers.
  • Financial Data Providers: Tools requiring live data (such as currency conversion rates) query third-party financial APIs.

These third-party providers may collect connection data in accordance with their own respective privacy policies.

3. Advertising and Cookies

Utiliverse uses third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' uses of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Data Retention

Because we do not require user accounts or database profiles to use our standard utilities, we do not actively store your personal data long-term. Any connection logs retained for security and operational purposes are routinely purged.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
