What appears in this log?

The goal of this page is to show ongoing curation without turning every small code edit into a public announcement. New utilities, major formula changes, important source updates, browser compatibility fixes, meaningful accessibility changes, navigation improvements, and corrections belong here.

Minor copy edits, formatting cleanup, dependency housekeeping, and changes that do not materially affect a visitor's experience may be omitted.

How dates are used

Dates describe the release or review period for the public-facing change. When a tool depends on a changing external registry, rule set, or service, the individual tool page can also carry its own “Last reviewed” note so visitors do not have to infer freshness from the site-wide log.

Corrections and revisions

If a formula, explanation, source interpretation, or technical behavior is found to be materially incorrect, Utiliverse should correct the page and record a meaningful correction here when the change could affect how users interpret a result.

Questions or correction suggestions can be sent through the Contact page. The broader review approach is documented in Editorial & Tool Methodology.
